Coordinated omission — service time vs the truth

Most benchmarks measure service time: how long an item took once work began. When a system stalls, the items queued behind the stall are measured late or not at all, and the stall disappears. Corrected latency measures from each item's scheduled arrival, so a stall is charged to everything behind it. Drive the load slider past 100% and watch the two diverge.
